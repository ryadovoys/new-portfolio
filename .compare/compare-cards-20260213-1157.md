### About me
Sergey Ryadovoy, VP of Design with 15 years across branding, product design, and AI-enabled experiences. I combine visual craft, systems thinking, and technical depth to lead products from concept to execution.

### Visa Product Design
This project supported Visa's 2022 digital rebrand rollout at global scale. I was a Lead Designer on the team that rebuilt the global Figma design system and replaced Sketch workflows, helping teams ship consistent, accessible experiences faster. In close collaboration with strategists, researchers, and copywriters, we delivered 50+ landing pages and campaigns while exploring future-facing concepts for Visa's digital presence.

### Digitas AI
Digitas AI is an internal platform for orchestrating AI agents across teams. I led experience design from workflow definition to interface design, then worked closely with engineers through implementation.

### Conversational AI
Conversational AI has been a core part of my recent work. I design chat flows, response behavior, and trust patterns so text interfaces feel clear and useful.

### Global Brands
I've worked with brands including Visa, Sephora, Delta, Amway, Mondelez, L'Oreal, and RaceTrac. The work spans products, interfaces, campaigns, and immersive experiences across different industries.

### Prototyping
Prototyping is central to how I work. I use Figma Make and Claude Code with Figma MCP for fast iteration, then refine interaction and motion details in Figma before handoff.

### Amway Website
This project redesigned Amway's digital experience across site architecture, component libraries, and visual design. I directed the work and led system decisions to keep global products consistent. Working with cross-functional teams, we built a scalable foundation for ongoing product and content updates.

### Generative Media
Generative media is part of my daily workflow for concepting and production. I use Google Nano Banana Pro, Midjourney, Visual Electric, Qwen, Kling, Wan, and Sora, plus custom pipelines in ComfyUI and Weavy.

### Motion Design
Motion design is a core craft in my product work. I use Figma for quick interaction studies and After Effects plus Cavalry for advanced motion systems and assets.

### Design Systems
Design systems are one of my core strengths. I build components, tokens, variables, and documentation that help teams ship consistent products at scale.

### AI Workflows
AI workflows are part of how I run design end to end. I use Claude Code, Cursor, Antigravity, and MCP tools across research, ideation, prototyping, design execution, and implementation support.

### Mindcomplete
Mindcomplete is an AI-powered writing assistant built as a Figma plugin. I created it to test real-time text continuation that keeps writing flow and tone consistent. The experiment explores how AI support can feel useful without taking over the process.

### UI UX
UI/UX is where visual craft meets product logic in my work. I've led design for websites, apps, dashboards, and systems across Visa, Amway, RaceTrac, and Delta.

### Branding
Branding is the foundation of my design practice. Over 15 years, I've built identity systems, visual languages, and brand guidelines for startups and larger organizations.

### Building Tools
Building small tools is a regular part of my practice. I create iOS apps, Figma plugins, web tools, and shortcuts to solve real workflow problems.

### Journely
Journely is an iPad app exploring handwriting-first conversational journaling. I created and designed the experience to test how handwritten input can drive conversational guidance. The project blends interaction design, writing flows, and visual craft for everyday use.

### Public Talks
Public speaking is an ongoing part of my work in AI and design. I share practical experiments, tools, and workflows teams can apply right away.

### RaceTrac App
A full redesign of the RaceTrac app, including a complete interface overhaul, a rebuilt rewards experience, and a new design system. I was the Lead Designer on the pitch design that won the project and then led the redesign execution.

### 36 Days of Type
36 Days of Type is a creative coding experiment. I animated every letter using OpenFrameworks and AI to explore how code and visual design can shape each other. It was a hands-on study in rhythm, form, and procedural interaction.

### Plantlife
PlantLife is a social platform for plant lovers to connect, shop, and share expertise. I helped shape the project from early concept, leading UI design, design system foundations, and brand communications. The work aligned community, commerce, and content into one cohesive product experience.

### Logo Design
Logo design is a long-term craft area in my career, with 100+ logos designed across industries. That work sharpened my focus on clarity, form, and recognizability.

### Packaging Design
Packaging design taught me to design for materials, production constraints, and shelf impact. My work was featured in The Dieline and Packaging of the World.

### Visa Analytics Platform
A redesign of Visa Analytics Platform, a self-service analytics product for financial institutions. I was a Lead Designer focused on clearer dashboards, peer benchmarking flows, and reporting experiences.

### Peace Sans Free Font
Peace Sans is a personal type design project I created and released for free. I designed and published the typeface, which reached 500K+ downloads on Behance.
